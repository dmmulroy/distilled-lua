# Task 25: S3 (`@distilled.cloud/s3`, 112 operations)

Read `tasks/10-parity-overview.md` and `STATUS.md` first, especially sections 14 (sigv4),
15 (xml) and 17 (aws: protocols, credentials; merge main often, task 17 runs in
parallel and owns `lua/distilled/aws/`). Distilled's S3 is its own package:
`../distilled/packages/s3/src/` (protocol.ts, protocols/, endpoint.ts, rules-engine/,
presign.ts, hash/, middleware/, eventstream/, sigv4.ts). It has no convert script: find
where its model/service definitions come from and how they are generated.

## Requirements

1. Port the S3 protocol faithfully: addressing styles, region redirects, `x-amz-content-sha256`
   (unsigned/streaming where Distilled does), checksums, ListObjectsV2 pagination,
   GetObject bodies (no streaming: whole body string), presigned URLs, error XML.
   Reuse task 17's credentials chain and task 14's signer.
2. Request/response conformance against the TS package for a sample of operations.
